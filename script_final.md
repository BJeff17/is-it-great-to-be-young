# Final Spoken Script — *Is it really that great to be young?*

> **Cible** : `6m25 – 6m35` (~800 mots parlés + pauses) · **12 slides** · Lis à voix posée (~125 mots/min).  
> Les repères entre crochets `[...]` indiquent uniquement tes clics, respirations et mouvements.

---

## 01 — The Hook · `0:00 → 0:45`
Every time you talk to an adult, they tell you the exact same thing: *"Enjoy your youth—these are the best years of your life!"* They say it with so much nostalgia, like being young is an all-inclusive vacation with zero stress.

`[PAUSE 2s · CLIC → slash "Really?"]`

Wait... are they really? Is it actually that great to be young?

Today, I want to look at this question from two completely different angles. First, how brutally hard it can feel. And second, how incredible it could—and should—be.

---

## 02 — Act I.A · Out of Sync · `0:45 → 1:30`
Because before it gets great, let's be honest about the weight.

Let's start with the personal part. In middle school, I was that kid who was just... too young. Not just younger on paper—out of sync with everyone else.

`[VOIX PLUS DOUCE · CLIC → Out of sync.]`

Picture standing at the edge of the school courtyard while groups around you are already laughing, trading inside jokes, moving like they all secretly read the same social handbook. And you? Zero friends. Just pretending to check your backpack so nobody sees you're alone.

---

## 03 — Act I.A · No Armor Yet · `1:30 → 2:00`
`[CLIC → No armor yet.]`

That's the thing people forget about being young: you don't have emotional armor yet. You haven't built the thick skin adults take for granted.

So when you're twelve and you feel out of place, it doesn't feel like a small chapter. It feels like the entire universe has quietly closed its door—because that tiny schoolyard IS your entire universe.

---

## 04 — Act I.B · Too Early · `2:00 → 2:40`
`[SE REDRESSER · TON PLUS AFFIRMÉ]`

And then, you grow a little. You finally find your footing, you lift your head... and you see the world waiting for you. A world moving faster and hitting harder than ever.

`[CLIC → 38% INTERRUPTED]`

Ours is a *"too early"* generation. We didn't get to ease into adulthood—our carefree years got interrupted halfway through.

`[CLIC → 07:02 / 07:03 / "This is fine."]`

You wake up at seven, grab your coffee, and scroll through three global crises before breakfast, while everyone asks you to pick your entire career before you even know who you are.

---

## 05 — Act I.B · The Bill · `2:40 → 3:15`
`[CLIC → The bill.]`

Honestly, it feels like sitting down at a restaurant just as the previous guests walk out the door—and the waiter turns around and hands US the receipt.

`[CLIC → FIX IT.]`

Climate crisis, unstable economy, broken systems... We are expected to solve every single mess our predecessors caused, while somehow building our own lives from scratch on shaky ground. We didn't even order—and yet, the bill is on our table.

---

## 06 — Act II · The Pivot · `3:15 → 3:45`
`[PIANO : silence · SILENCE 3s · 2 PAS SUR LE CÔTÉ]`

Looking at all of that... you might wonder why anyone would ever want to be young.

And yet—pause for a second.

`[CLIC → Unwritten pages. · PIANO : accord doré]`

If being young is so heavy, why would every billionaire on earth trade every penny they own just to be our age again? Because being young IS the big deal. We hold the one thing money can never buy back: unwritten pages.

---

## 07 — Act III.A · Plot Development · `3:45 → 4:30`
`[SOURIRE · GESTES OUVERTS · CLIC → Plot Development.]`

Because being young isn't just about the weight you carry—it's about what you're still allowed to do with your time.

It's about having fun without needing an excuse, even in a serious world. At forty-five, a mistake comes with a mortgage and real consequences. At twenty? You can try something crazy, fall off the board, laugh in the dust, and nobody calls it failing. You get to call it learning. Every awkward phase, every detour—it's all just plot development.

---

## 08 — Act III.B · 01 What to Love · `4:30 → 4:55`
`[CLIC → 01 What to love.]`

And while nothing is locked in stone yet, you get to answer the real questions.

First: figuring out what to love. Which curiosity keeps you awake at midnight. Which art, which ideas, which weird projects actually make you feel alive instead of just looking impressive on a résumé.

---

## 09 — Act III.B · 02 How to Love · `4:55 → 5:15`
`[CLIC → 02 How to love.]`

Second: learning how to love. Remember that lonely kid standing in the courtyard? Youth is also when you finally meet your people—the friends who match your energy—and when you learn how to show up for them honestly, deeply, without holding back.

---

## 10 — Act III.B · 03 Zero Regrets · `5:15 → 5:35`
`[CLIC → 03 Zero regrets.]`

And third: learning how to live by your own rules, in a way that won't make you look back one day wishing you had been braver. Taking the risk before life decides for you. Not someone else's checklist—yours.

---

## 11 — Finale · The Paradox · `5:35 → 6:00`
So yes—being young really is the big deal.

`[CLIC → Short enough to regret.]`

But just like every great deal in life, it comes with an expiration date. It's something long enough to let you truly enjoy it, explore, and fall in love with the world... yet short enough to leave you with plenty of regrets if you let it slip by on autopilot.

---

## 12 — Finale · Your Youth List · `6:00 → 6:30`
`[RETOUR AU CENTRE · CLIC → …and just be happy.]`

So if you are still standing in those great ages right now, don't just rush to the finish line.

Take a real break from the noise. Write a list—not only the huge ambitious goals, but the small terrifying things you've always wanted to try, the nights you don't want to miss. Manage to get through it... and just be happy.

`[SILENCE 1.5s · REGARD PUBLIC]`

Thank you.
