## Détail Slide par Slide (Texte · Régie · Transitions)

### 01 — Ouverture : Le Cliché vs La Réalité (`0:00 – 0:45`)
- **Visuel** : Fond noir 35mm, citation *“The best years of your life.”* (`Beat 0`) → trait rouge vif + *Really?* (`Beat 1`).
- **Scène & Voix** : Immobile au centre. Regard calme et léger sourire complice au départ, puis pause réelle de 2 secondes juste après la citation pour laisser le public réagir au trait rouge.
- **Musique** : Notes d'ouverture feutrées dès la levée du rideau.
- **Script (~70 mots)** :
  > Every time you talk to an adult, they tell you the exact same thing: *"Enjoy your youth—these are the best years of your life!"* They say it with so much nostalgia, like being young is an all-inclusive vacation.  
  > `[PAUSE 2s · CLIC]`  
  > Wait... are they really? Is it actually that great to be young? Today, I want to look at this question from two different angles: first, why carrying your twenties right now can feel heavy; and second, how incredible being young could—and should—be.

  > `[CLIC → Unwritten pages.]`  
  > If being young is really that heavy, why would every billionaire on earth trade every penny they own just to stand in our shoes? Because being young IS the big deal. We hold the one thing money can never buy back: unwritten pages.
